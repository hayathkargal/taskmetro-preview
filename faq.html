LET’S CLEAR THINGS UP

Good questions.
Thoughtful answers.

A little clarity goes a long way. Here’s what to know before we take on your Bengaluru task.

Can I book from outside Bengaluru or India?+

Yes. TaskMetro helps NRIs, people living in other cities, businesses and local residents coordinate tasks in Bengaluru. Share your requirement and we will confirm feasibility.

What will my task cost?+

The base fee is ₹199, plus runner time at ₹249 per hour billed in 30-minute increments. Travel, parking, institutional fees and any special handling are additional. You receive a quote before work begins.

How will I know what is happening?+

We share updates through your preferred contact channel, with photos, receipts and a summary of the agreed work. GPS logs are shared where relevant.

Can you complete a task the same day?+

Urgent requests are assessed against runner availability, opening hours and third-party requirements. Same-day completion is not guaranteed, and urgent tasks may have additional charges.

Do you handle original or sensitive documents?+

Document handling is agreed in advance, including authorization, identity checks and sealed handovers where needed. Special handling charges may apply. Do not send passwords, OTPs or unnecessary sensitive records in the request form.

Do I need to be present for official work?+

Some government offices, banks and institutions require your presence or specific authorization. We check which collection, submission or coordination steps can be handled on your behalf.

How do payment and refunds work?+

The base fee initiates planning and is non-refundable once planning begins. Runner time, travel and third-party charges follow the agreed quote. UPI and bank transfer are available. See the Terms of Service for refund and cancellation conditions.

Which areas do you cover?+

We cover Bengaluru urban limits. For outskirts or nearby cities, share the location so we can assess feasibility and travel costs.

Can you help with recurring or business tasks?+

Yes. Recurring errands, property checks and corporate task coordination can be discussed for a tailored quote. Tell us the frequency, scope and locations.

Do you provide legal, financial or medical advice?+

TaskMetro provides errand facilitation, authorized paperwork coordination and logistics. We do not provide legal, financial or medical advisory services, or replace emergency services.
