All questions
Can I book from outside Bengaluru or India?+

Yes. TaskMetro helps NRIs, people living in other cities, businesses and local residents coordinate tasks in Bengaluru. Share your requirement and we will confirm feasibility.

What will my task cost?+

The base fee is ₹199, plus runner time at ₹249 per hour billed in 30-minute increments. Travel, parking, institutional fees and any special handling are additional. You receive a quote before work begins.

How will I know what is happening?+

We share updates through your preferred contact channel, with photos, receipts and a summary of the agreed work. GPS logs are shared where relevant.

Can you complete a task the same day?+

Urgent requests are assessed against runner availability, opening hours and third-party requirements. Same-day completion is not guaranteed, and urgent tasks may have additional charges.
